# Frequently Asked Questions

In case you find that your question is not covered here, please write us an email at [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in), or ask us on our [Zulip](https://zulip.scipy.in) chat.

## Attending

:::{dropdown} Do I need to know the Python programming language?

Yes and no. Most sessions will assume you write code in Python or an allied programming language for computational and scientific work, not that you use any particular language. Some workshops do expect Python specifically, and each one states its prerequisites.
:::

:::{dropdown} Do I need to be a researcher or work at a university?

Nope! The conference is open to anyone, whether in academia, industry, or independently. We welcome students, hobbyists, and professionals alike. The conference is about scientific computing and data science, and we expect attendees to be interested in those topics.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I attend just one day of the conference?

Yes. We plan to sell a single-day ticket for the workshop day on Saturday, a ticket for the
conference day on Sunday, and a combined ticket for both days. See [registration](register).
:::

:::{dropdown} When does the registration open?

We will announce the opening of registrations and ticket sales on our [news page](news/index) and on our social media channels. Please keep an eye out for updates.
:::

:::{dropdown} What language is the conference in?

The conference is conducted in English. All sessions, including talks, workshops, and discussions, will be in English. We encourage attendees to communicate in English to ensure that everyone can participate, understand, and contribute to the discussions. If you have any concerns about language accessibility, please reach out to us at [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in).
:::

:::{dropdown} What time does each day start and finish?

You may plan for a full working day on both days. The conference will start in the morning at 08:30 AM IST and finish in the evening at 06:00 PM IST. The exact schedule will be published on the [programme page](programme) once it is finalised.
:::

:::{dropdown} Is food included?

Yes. We plan to provide lunch and light refreshments, including tea/coffee on both days.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can you cater for my dietary requirements?

We plan to ask about this on the registration form. Please let us know if you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, and we will do our best to accommodate them. We cannot guarantee that we can meet all dietary requirements, but we will make every effort to provide suitable options for attendees with specific needs.
:::

:::{dropdown} Is there a dress code?

No. You may wear what you would wear to work or to a lab, or casual attire. Chennai in December is warm and humid, and rooms with air conditioning can be a good deal colder than outside, so something with sleeves is worth carrying. We recommend comfortable clothing and footwear suitable for walking around the campus and attending sessions.
:::

:::{dropdown} Should I bring a laptop?
For the workshop day, yes, and it is close to essential. For the conference day, it is optional. You may want to bring a laptop for taking notes, following along with presentations, or participating in interactive sessions. However, it is not mandatory for attending the conference day. It is required to bring a laptop if you are speaking. We recommend that you bring your own laptop with the necessary software and tools installed for the workshops you plan to attend. Please check the workshop prerequisites and setup instructions in advance. Please also carry any accessories you may need, such as chargers, adapters, headphones, portable storage devices, HDMI ports, or any other equipment required for your participation in the workshops or presentations.
:::

:::{dropdown} Will there be Wi-Fi?
We expect to have Wi-Fi at the venue. We recommend having a mobile connection as a fallback, particularly if you are presenting or following along in a workshop.
:::

:::{dropdown} Is there help with the ticket price?
Yes. See [financial aid](financial-aid). We are still working out what it covers and how much we have, which depends on what our sponsorship will bring in this year. We will announce the details on our [news page](news/index) and on our social media channels once we have a clearer picture of our budget.
:::

:::{dropdown} Will I get a certificate of participation?
We will confirm this before registration opens. If you need one for your institution, please inform us at [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in) so that we can consider it in our planning and accommodate your request.
:::

## Tickets

:::{dropdown} How much is a ticket?
The ticket price is not set yet. We are keeping prices low to make the conference accessible. We will announce the ticket prices on our [news page](news/index) and on our social media channels once they are finalised. Please keep an eye out for updates.
:::

:::{dropdown} What does a ticket cover?
A ticket gives you access to the sessions on the days your ticket covers, lunch and refreshments, and conference materials. It does not cover travel or accommodation.
:::

:::{dropdown} My employer is paying. Is there a different ticket?
Yes. If your institution or company is covering your attendance, please buy a corporate ticket. It costs a little more, and it is what lets us keep the standard ticket affordable for students and independent researchers.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I get an invoice or a receipt for reimbursement?
Yes. We will explain how on the [registration page](register) once we have picked a ticketing platform.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I get a refund or pass my ticket to someone else?
We have not settled the policy yet. It will be on the [registration page](register) before tickets go on sale.
:::

## Workshops

:::{dropdown} How do I choose which workshop to attend?
Places in each workshop are limited, so we will ask you to pick one when the programme is out. We will announce how that works alongside the [schedule](schedule). Since the workshops will be conducted in parallel, you will be able to attend two workshops on the Saturday, one in the morning and one in the afternoon. Please read the workshop descriptions and prerequisites carefully to choose the ones that best match your interests and skill level.
:::

:::{dropdown} Do I need to install anything beforehand?
Usually, yes. Each workshop will publish its own setup instructions and prerequisites in advance. Please work through them before you arrive!
:::

:::{dropdown} Are the workshops suitable for beginners?
It depends on the workshop. All workshops will state their prerequisites and skill level in the workshop description. Some workshops are designed for beginners, while others may require prior experience or knowledge in specific areas. Please read the workshop descriptions carefully to determine if a workshop is suitable for your skill level.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I attend the workshops without attending the conference day?
Yes. A workshop-day ticket covers Saturday on its own.
:::

## Watching online

:::{dropdown} Can I watch the conference remotely?
Yes. We will stream the conference day on Sunday on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@scipy-india). You do not need a ticket or an account to watch the proceedings online. The livestream will be free and open to all.
:::

:::{dropdown} Are the workshops streamed?
No. Workshops are in person only.
:::

:::{dropdown} What time zone are the times in?
India Standard Time, which is UTC+5:30.
:::

:::{dropdown} Will talks be recorded?
Yes, and we will publish them on our YouTube channel afterwards. If you are a speaker and would prefer not to be recorded, please tell us and we will keep your session off the stream.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can online viewers ask questions?
We would like to make this work and we will confirm nearer the date. It depends on what the audio-visual setup in the auditorium allows.
:::

## Speaking

:::{dropdown} When is the call for proposals opening?
The dates are on the ["Important dates" table](index), and we will announce the opening on
the [news page](news/index). The [call for proposals](cfp) will open with more information.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I submit if I have not spoken at a conference before?
Yes, we love first-time speakers! We will work with you to solidify your proposal if it is selected. Based on interest, we may also run a speaker mentorship programme to help you submit a strong proposal. For selected speakers, the editorial board will provide guidance and support to help you prepare for your talk or workshop through mock presentations. We want to make sure that everyone has a positive experience and feels confident in their presentation.
:::

:::{dropdown} How long is a talk or a workshop?
The length of a talk will be 30 minutes, including time for questions. Workshops will be 3 hours long, with a short break in the middle. We will provide more details on the [call for proposals](cfp) page.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I submit more than one proposal, or co-present?
Yes to both. Please submit each proposal separately, and name every presenter on your proposal. If you are co-presenting, please ensure that all presenters are available for the session and that you have coordinated your presentation accordingly.
:::

:::{dropdown} Do speakers get a free ticket?
We will confirm this when we publish the call for proposals.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I present remotely or send a pre-recorded talk?
We are planning an in-person programme, and we will not be able to accommodate remote presentations or pre-recorded talks. We encourage all speakers to attend in person to engage with the audience and participate in discussions.
:::

:::{dropdown} How do I give a lightning talk?
Lightning talks do not go through the call for proposals. We will explain how to sign up, and confirm whether there is a lightning talk session, when the [programme](programme) is out.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I present a poster?
A poster session is [tentative](programme). If it goes ahead, we will announce it before the date.
:::

:::{dropdown} Is speaker travel covered?
Speakers can apply for [financial aid](financial-aid), either when they submit or after we accept the proposal. Whether we can provide it depends on our sponsorship and budget, and we will confirm the details when we open the financial aid application process.
:::

:::{dropdown} Will slides be published?
Yes, we require that all slides be made available under a permissive license, and we will publish them on our website after the conference. We encourage speakers to share their slides and any accompanying materials to benefit the wider community.
:::

## Getting there and staying

:::{dropdown} Where exactly is the conference?
IIT Madras, Chennai 600036. The workshops on Saturday are at the Department of
Physics and the conference day on Sunday is at the IC&SR Building, about 400
metres apart. The [venue and travel page](venue) has directions.
:::

:::{dropdown} How do I get to the campus from the airport or the railway station?
The [venue and travel page](venue) has routes by taxi, bus, and metro from the
airport and from both main railway stations.
:::

:::{dropdown} Do I need identification to get onto campus?
We are confirming what campus entry involves, including which gate to use, and we
will publish it on the [venue page](venue). We will issue a conference badge to all
attendees at the registration desk.
:::

:::{dropdown} Where should I stay?
We will list options at a range of prices near the campus on the
[venue page](venue). As we get closer to the conference, we will also try to arrange a block booking at a guest house on campus or a nearby hotel. Any nearby hotels worth considering will be listed on the [venue page](venue). You are welcome to make your own arrangements, but please book early if you are coming from outside Chennai.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can you send a letter of invitation for a visa application?
Yes. Please see the [venue and travel page](venue) for details on how to request a letter of invitation. We will provide a letter of invitation for visa applications, shall you require one.
:::

:::{dropdown} Is the venue accessible?
We are in the process of checking step-free access, lifts, accessible restrooms, and child care at the venue and we will publish what we find on the [venue page](venue). If you have a specific accessibility requirement, please write to us pre-emptively at [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in) so that we can make arrangements to accommodate your needs.
:::

:::{dropdown} What is the weather like in December?
Warm and humid, with mild evenings and the occasional passing shower! Please see the [venue page](venue).
:::

## Sponsorship and volunteering

:::{dropdown} How does my company sponsor?
Please write to [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in). We are happy to discuss sponsorship options and how your company can support the conference. We will provide details on sponsorship packages, benefits, and opportunities for visibility and engagement with the conference attendees. We welcome sponsors from various industries who share our commitment to scientific computing and data science.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I sponsor as an individual?
Yes. Please write to [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in), and say if you want your
contribution earmarked for [financial aid](financial-aid) or for general sponsorship. We are happy to discuss sponsorship options and how you can support the conference as an individual. Your contribution will help us make the conference more accessible and inclusive for attendees from diverse backgrounds.
:::

:::{dropdown} Can I volunteer for the conference?
Yes! We are looking for volunteers to join the programme committee and help with various aspects of the conference. We plan to open a volunteer form soon, and we will announce it on our [news page](news/index) and on our social media channels. Please keep an eye out for updates. We welcome volunteers with a range of skills and experience, and we value diversity and inclusivity in our volunteer team!
:::

:::{dropdown} How can I help spread the word?
You may post about us somewhere we are not already reaching, such as a lab mailing list, a department, or a local user group. You may also share our [news](news/index) posts on social media, or write about us on your own blog. We are happy to provide graphics and text for you to use in your posts.
:::

## Anything else

:::{dropdown} Is there a Code of Conduct?
Yes. It applies to both days, the stream, and social events around the conference and all activities of the conference and community, in person and online. Please refer to the [Code of Conduct](coc) page for details.
:::

:::{dropdown} Who runs the conference?
The SciPy India community, through the [programme committee](team). Afrost, the
Association for Promotion of Free and Open Source Technologies, is our organising
partner and fiscal host.
:::

:::{dropdown} How is this related to the other SciPy conferences?
SciPy India is its conference with its organisers, run in the same spirit as the SciPy conferences elsewhere such as SciPy US, EuroSciPy, SciPy Japan and SciPyData Japan, SciPy Latam, and SciPy Korea, and several PyData conferences and meetups all around the planet. Please refer to https://scipy.org/community/#conferences for more. This is the fourteenth edition, and the first
since 2021. For more details, see [past editions](https://scipy.in/past-editions).
:::

:::{dropdown} How do I keep in touch afterwards?
You may join us on [Zulip](https://zulip.scipy.in), where the community carries on between
conferences. Thank you for your interest!
:::

:::{dropdown} My question is not answered here. Who may I reach out to?

Write to [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in) or ask on [Zulip](https://zulip.scipy.in). If it is a question other people are likely to have, we will add it to this page.
:::
