# Registrations

Tickets for the SciPy India 2026 Conference are now on sale on KonfHub. Tickets are required for in-person attendance, and we have a limited number of them available, so please book early to secure your spot!

```{button-link} https://konfhub.com/scipy-india-2026
:color: primary
:class: sci-new-tab

Buy your tickets
```

By buying a ticket, you hereby agree to our [terms and conditions](terms). In short: all sales are final with no refunds or transfers, attendees must be 18 or older, and you will need to show your ticket and a valid ID at the registration desk.

Early bird prices are available until **19th October 2026**. Regular prices apply from 20th October to 8th December, and late bird prices from 9th to 18th December, subject to availability. - **Corporate:** if your attendance is supported by your institution or company, please sign up for a corporate ticket at slightly higher pricing. This helps us keep the conference accessible to students, independent researchers, and those from underrepresented groups.
- **Contributor:** a both-days ticket at a higher price for those who would like to support the conference beyond the cost of their own attendance. Thank you!

If you are unable to attend in person, we will publish recordings of the conference day talks on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@scipy-india) after the conference.

## What a ticket covers

A ticket to the SciPy India 2026 Conference covers the following:

- Access to all conference talks, workshops, and the poster session (in-person only)
- Conference materials (programme, schedule, and any digital resources)
- Networking opportunities with fellow attendees, speakers, and sponsors
- Meals: lunch and coffee/tea breaks on the workshop and conference days

We do not provide accommodation or travel support as part of the ticket. Please refer to our [venue and travel](venue) page for information on nearby hotels and transportation options.

## Invoices and receipts

You will receive an email with a receipt and an invoice after your purchase. If you need anything further, please write to [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in).

## Recordings

We plan to publish recordings of the conference day talks on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@scipy-india) after the conference.

Workshops are in person only and will not be recorded. If you are interested in attending a workshop, please register for the conference and book a ticket.

If you have any questions, please refer to our [FAQ](faq) page. In case your question is not addressed there, please write to [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in).
