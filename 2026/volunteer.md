# Volunteer

We are looking to form a programme committee for the SciPy India 2026 conference. The committee is made up of volunteers who help with the conference in various ways, from reviewing proposals to running sessions on the day. We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds and levels of experience.

:::{admonition} The call for volunteers is not open yet!
:class: note

We are going to open the call for volunteers soon! Please watch our [news](news/index) page and our social media for updates.
:::

In the meantime, if you would like to help, please write to [info@scipy.in](mailto:info@scipy.in) or introduce yourself on [Zulip](https://zulip.scipy.in), where we conduct most of our activities publicly. You are welcome to get in touch even if you are not yet sure how you would like to contribute!
