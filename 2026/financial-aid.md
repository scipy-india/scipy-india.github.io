# Financial aid

We plan to disburse financial aid towards the proceedings of the conference. This is to help people who would otherwise be unable to attend the conference, and to encourage participation from groups underrepresented at events of this kind.

We are in the process of working hard to provide financial aid for the conference ticket, visa fees (as applicable), travel within India. The amount of aid and the number of recipients depends on our proceeds from our sponsors, and we will be able to provide more information once we have a clearer picture of our budget.

:::{admonition} Applications are not open yet
:class: note

Conference registrations and financial aid applications will open closer to the conference date. Please keep an eye on our [news](news/index) page for updates!
:::

## Who can apply

- Students and early-career researchers
- People who are not currently employed
- People travelling to Chennai at their own expense
- Speakers whose institution or employer does not cover travel expenses
- People from groups underrepresented at scientific conferences of this kind, who would otherwise be unable to attend
- People who are not in any of the above categories but have a compelling reason to attend and cannot afford it

While we cannot guarantee that we will be able to provide financial aid to everyone who applies, we will do our best to support as many people as possible within our budget.

When in doubt, apply! We will consider all applications on a case-by-case basis.

## Review process

A small group of organisers reads the applications and we do not share them beyond
that group. Applying for aid, or receiving it, has no bearing on whether we accept
your proposal, and the [programme committee](team) does not see financial aid
applications.

## Funding it

Are you a sponsor or an individual who would like to support the conference? Or, do you work for an organisation that would like to sponsor the conference? Please see the [sponsorship page](sponsor) for details on how to contribute. We can earmark a contribution for financial aid if you ask.
