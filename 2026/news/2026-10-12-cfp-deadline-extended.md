# We have extended the CFP deadline

We have extended the call for proposals (CFP) for the SciPy India 2026 Conference. You can now submit talks, workshops, and posters until **1st November 2026, 23:59 IST**.

Send your proposal through the [submission portal](https://cfp.scipy.in/). We welcome first-time speakers, so if you have an idea you have been sitting on, send it in.
