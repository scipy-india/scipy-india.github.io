# The call for proposals is now open

The call for proposals (CFP) for the SciPy India 2026 Conference is now open! We are looking for **30-minute talks**, **3-hour hands-on workshops**, and **posters** on scientific computing and research software across eight exciting tracks.

Please read the [call for proposals](https://cfp.scipy.in/scipy-india-2026/cfp) for the session formats, tracks, and guidance on writing a strong proposal, and then [submit your proposal](https://cfp.scipy.in/scipy-india-2026/cfp) through our submission portal.

The CFP closes on **19th October 2026, 23:59 IST**. We review proposals on a rolling basis, so we recommend submitting early.

You do not need to be a core maintainer or a seasoned speaker to submit, and we warmly welcome first-time speakers. If you would like guidance on preparing your proposal, please say hello on [Zulip](https://zulip.scipy.in).
