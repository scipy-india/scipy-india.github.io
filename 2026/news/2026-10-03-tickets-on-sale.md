# Tickets are now on sale

Registration for the SciPy India 2026 Conference is open! Tickets are on sale on [KonfHub](https://konfhub.com/scipy-india-2026) for the workshops on Saturday, 19th December 2026, the conference day on Sunday, 20th December 2026, or both, at IIT Madras, Chennai.

```{button-link} https://konfhub.com/scipy-india-2026
:color: primary
:class: sci-new-tab

Buy your tickets
```

## Early bird prices

Early bird tickets start at **₹699** for a single day and **₹1,199** for both days, and students pay the same. These prices are available until **19th October 2026**, and the number of tickets is limited, so please book early.

| Ticket category | Conference day only | Workshop day only | Both days |
| :-------------- | ------------------: | ----------------: | --------: |
| Early bird      |                ₹699 |              ₹699 |    ₹1,199 |
| Student         |                ₹699 |              ₹699 |    ₹1,199 |
| Corporate       |              ₹2,499 |            ₹2,499 |    ₹3,999 |
| Contributor     |       Not available |     Not available |    ₹3,999 |

Goods and Services Tax (GST) and KonfHub's platform fees are added at checkout. The full price table, including the regular and late bird prices, is on the [registration page](../register).

## Contributor tickets

If you would like to support the conference beyond the cost of your own attendance, we have a **contributor ticket** for both days at ₹3,999. It is what lets us keep the standard ticket affordable for students and independent researchers, and we are very grateful for it.

If your institution or company is paying for your attendance, please pick the **corporate ticket** instead.

## What a ticket covers

A ticket gives you access to the sessions on the days it covers, lunch and refreshments on those days, and the conference materials. It does not cover travel or accommodation. Please see the [venue and travel page](../venue) for directions and places to stay.

By buying a ticket, you agree to our [terms and conditions](../terms). In short: all sales are final, with no refunds or transfers, attendees must be 18 or older, and you will need to show your ticket and a valid ID at the registration desk.
