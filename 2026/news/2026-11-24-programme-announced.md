# The programme is out

The programme for the SciPy India 2026 Conference is now on the {doc}`programme page </programme>`: 16 talks and seven posters for the conference day on Sunday, 20th December, and five workshops on Saturday, 19th December, at IIT Madras, Chennai. Every selected speaker has confirmed.

One of the workshops is an {ref}`invited workshop <invited-workshop>` on synthetic aperture radar (SAR) imagery, from Sentinel-1 to NISAR, a special collaboration between SciPy India 2026 and the Alaska Satellite Facility at the University of Alaska Fairbanks, with Shubham Sharma, Julia White, and Alex Lewandowski.

Our third keynote speaker is {doc}`Usha Rengaraju </news/2026-11-25-keynote-usha-rengaraju>`, with *Beyond Deep Learning: JAX as a Scientific Computing Engine*, joining the keynotes by Dharhas Pothina and Venkatesh Raghavan.

We will publish the schedule, with the time and room of each session, in early December.

Thank you to everyone who sent us a proposal. We received far more than two days can hold, and choosing among them was the hardest part of organising this conference so far!
