## About FOSS United

FOSS United is a Section 8 non-profit organisation that aims to promote and strengthen the free and open source software (FOSS) ecosystem in India. SciPy India is incubated by FOSS United, and our community calls and our devroom and booth at IndiaFOSS have all happened with their support.

You can learn more at [fossunited.org](https://fossunited.org), and follow FOSS United on [GitHub](https://github.com/fossunited) and [Twitter/X](https://x.com/FOSSUnited).

## Thank you

We are very grateful to FOSS United for backing the conference, this year and from the very beginning. Grants and sponsorships cover the costs of running the conference and let us keep ticket prices low.

If your organisation would like to support the conference as well, please read our [sponsor page](../sponsor) or write to [sponsor@scipy.in](mailto:sponsor@scipy.in).
