## About OpenTeams

**Owned Intelligence. Built For Enterprise.**

Thousands of open source contributors built the foundations of modern AI. OpenTeams helps organizations deploy, support, and own that technology at enterprise scale.

You can learn more at [openteams.com](https://openteams.com), and follow OpenTeams on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/openteams/) and [X](https://x.com/openteamsinc).

## Thank you

We are very grateful to OpenTeams for their support. Sponsorship covers the costs of running the conference and lets us keep ticket prices low.

If your organisation would like to support the conference as well, please read our [sponsor page](../sponsor) or write to [sponsor@scipy.in](mailto:sponsor@scipy.in).
